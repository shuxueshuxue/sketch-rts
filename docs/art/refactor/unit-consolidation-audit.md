# Unit consolidation audit — recommendation, not implemented

This is a read-only rules audit requested after the art migration began. No unit stats, training lists, abilities, economy rules, AI routes or acceptance thresholds have been changed for this proposal. The art/performance comparison must finish against unchanged simulation rules before any consolidation lands.

Sources inspected (including the non-catalog behavior pass): `damage-types.ts`, `damage-reduction.ts`, `veteran-skills.ts`, `physical-body.ts`, `ship-cabin.ts`, `spark-fire.ts`, all unit-kind branches in `sim.ts`, and `src/shared/catalog.ts` (`UNIT_RULES`, `ABILITY_DEFS`, production buildings, tiers and upgrades); `attack-presentation.ts`; `equipment.ts`; `unit-abilities.ts`; `sim.ts` summon execution and autocast; `shop.ts`; `map.ts` and `generated-map.ts` mercenary offers; `camps.ts`; story variant rules. The existing upkeep rules are outside this proposal and must remain intact.

## Corrected recommendation: two caster merges only

Merge actual redundant battlefield roles, while keeping acquisition paths and authored faction appearances separate from unit identity. Prefer an existing canonical stat line over averaging every field or taking each donor's strongest numbers. The numerical selections below are explicit proposals, not claims of balance neutrality.

| Proposed canonical unit | Retire into it | Why this is a real duplicate | Proposed baseline and deliberate tradeoff |
|---|---|---|---|
| `summoner` | `pyreCaller` | Both cast the same summon with the same product, cooldown, lifetime, targeting and autocast. Their ordinary attack is a low-damage magic projectile. | Keep the existing summoner line: 95 HP, speed 56, damage 8 / 1.9 s, range 218.4, aim 400, gold 180, training 10.5 s, supply 2, tier 2. Pyre caller currently has 88 HP, speed 59, damage 7, range 208, aim 440, gold 174, training 9.5 s. Thus Ember gains 7 HP, 1 attack damage and 10.4 reach, but loses 3 speed and 40 aim speed, pays 6 more gold and waits 1 s longer. Do not combine the best of both. |
| `priest` | `emberAcolyte` | `heal` and `emberMend` are identical in every rule field: nonmechanical targets, 55 healing, 12 s cooldown, actual range 240, planner range 220, autocast on. Both units are slow, fragile ranged healers with supply 2 and tier 2. | Keep the priest line: 90 HP, speed 60, damage 7 / 1.8 s, range 201.6, aim 440, gold 135, training 9.25 s. Ember currently has 78 HP, speed 62, damage 6, range 192, aim 480, gold 130, training 8.75 s. This is a notable HP increase (+15.4%) with slower movement/aim and a higher price; explicitly revalidate Ember sustain. Keep healing at 55 / 12 s; do not increase throughput to compensate. |
The highest-confidence mechanical cleanup is also `cinderSoul → summon` and `emberMend → heal`. Do **not** give a merged unit both spell IDs: ability cooldowns are per ability, so two aliases in its ability list could permit duplicate independent casts. The merged unit must have exactly one authoritative ability.

Only the two caster merges above are recommended for implementation. An earlier catalog-only pass incorrectly proposed merging Spark Archer and Ash Warden; that proposal is withdrawn after following the actual damage, on-hit, veterancy and physical-body paths. Neither is just a statistical copy. The corrected findings below are authoritative.

## Non-catalog mechanics that prevent superficial merges

- **Spark Archer is the ignition source of the Ember composition.** `spark-fire.ts` specifies a deterministic 15% ignition chance, radius 38, lifetime 6 s and 1 damage every .5 s. `sim.ts` applies it on a land-unit hit outside a ship deck; the burning field excludes deck/sea units and applies `scorch` for 8 s to damaged units. The field can also damage buildings in its radius. Native shots use `FIRE_ARROW`, a physical ranged piercing hit with the fire element, while ordinary Archer is `RANGED_PIERCE` without fire. The visible attack may look like another arrow, but its rules are not the same.
- **Scorch is a real combined-arms mechanic.** `attackDamageAgainstTarget` gives Ember Ravager +7 damage and Cinder Runner +5 against a scorched target. Ash Hexer changes the target's outgoing-damage multiplier from .45 to .3 when scorched; Witch instead applies an unconditional .4. Removing Spark Archer or flattening these units into generic infantry/casters deletes this interaction.
- **Ash Warden uses piercing melee damage.** `UNIT_DAMAGE_PROFILES.ashWarden` and Lancer are `MELEE_PIERCE`, whereas Footman is `MELEE_CUT`. These are independent profile dimensions, not inferred from range or art, and can be matched by damage filters. Warden and Footman also differ in collision radius (19 versus 18) and default physical mass (about 142 kg versus Footman's explicit 105 kg), affecting deck load/cabin quota and physical interactions. Warden/Lancer/Footman share the `veteranPhalanx` and `veteranEndurance` specialist pool; Phalanx is not Warden-exclusive, but damage and physical differences still prevent calling the rows duplicates.
- **Ravager and Runner do not have the same progression.** In addition to their different speed/body profiles and scorch bonuses, Ravager can roll March and Endurance; Runner's specialist pool contains only March. These are actual gameplay choices after veterancy.
- **The two proposed caster pairs pass the same cross-check.** Priest/Acolyte both use `MAGIC_RANGED`, radius 16 and default body mass 85 kg, with the same Healing Wave / Inner Fire / Renewal pool. Summoner/Pyre Caller both use `MAGIC_RANGED`, radius 17 and about 102 kg, with the same Inner Fire / Renewal pool. None of the explicit on-hit or target-damage kind branches gives these pairs a separate intrinsic passive. Both pairs are walking land crew; the owned-unit equipment fallback permits both. One edge differs in `canEquip`: Priest is explicitly listed as humanoid even if neutral, while Acolyte relies on nonneutral ownership; normalization must decide whether neutral scripted instances retain that rare distinction.
- **Damage type and projectile art are separate fields.** Thorn Slinger currently has a stone-style projectile presentation but an explicit `RANGED_PIERCE` damage profile. Murloc Hunter uses spear presentation and `RANGED_PIERCE`. Neither decision authorizes changing damage because the new art uses a sling or javelin. Knight's innate profile remains `MELEE_CUT` even where mounted art uses a lance; art-only work does not silently rewrite that rule.
- **Even equal veteran pools are not identical rerolls.** The deterministic veteran-choice seed includes kind. Existing rolled choices must remain stored through identity migration rather than being regenerated under the canonical kind.

## Summoner findings in detail

`ABILITY_DEFS.summon` and `cinderSoul` are exactly the same behavior: range 260, planner range 240, cooldown 40 seconds, `summonKind: spirit`, duration 60 seconds, `effectType: summon`, autocast on. There is no separate fire summon. Both create the same 85 HP, speed 70, 13 damage / 1.2 s, melee reach 55 spirit, with supply 0 and XP reward 0.

A summons' location must be walkable or fit on a friendly ship deck with enough mass capacity. Its expiry tick is set explicitly and it begins idle. Autocast requires an enemy within 360 (260 + 100); it refuses another summon when a friendly unit of the same summon kind is within 320; otherwise it places the summon up to 60 towards the closest enemy. This shared-company gate means massed summoners do not simply multiply the naive `60 / 40 = 1.5` spirit uptime. Manual casts and different spatial deployments behave differently. A merge must preserve this logic.

Both curse variants inflict 100 immediate damage on expiring summons, more than an unbuffed spirit's 85 HP. Ash Chieftain's caster/summon slayer multiplier is another deliberate counter. Do not merge summoner into healer or witch: fielding extra bodies, restoring existing HP and suppressing damage have different counters, positions and resource effects.

## Production, acquisition and migration constraints

1. **Do not collapse production buildings as a side effect.** Summoner/Priest currently come from Grove Sanctum (175 gold, 11.25 s construction); their Ember equivalents come from Cinder Spire (170 gold, 10.75 s), which also produces the basic archer and researches speed/range/leadership. The separately retained basic archers/infantry continue to use their current faction buildings. Canonical unit identity must support explicit faction-specific production sources. The current `trainedAt` is a single building field and `BUILDING_DEFS.trains` derives from it; simply deleting the race or changing the field breaks one faction's production or changes its tech investment.
2. **Keep supply as technology.** Advanced units require supply capacity 42; elite units require 60. These are cap thresholds, not current usage. Merged Summoner/Priest stay tier 2, supply 2; the separately retained basic roster keeps its current gates. Preserve the upkeep rules completed in #314/#315.
3. **Do not accidentally upgrade neutrals and summons.** Global ordinary-combat upgrade lists derive from trainable kinds. Hired units, camp units and Spirit currently are outside that list. Merging their kinds into trained units would change upgrade eligibility as well as numbers.
4. **Mercenary offers are not the item shop.** `SHOP_GOODS` sells gear/consumables, not units or summoned spirits. Unit offers live in map mercenary camps, with `hireKind`, cost, stock and purchase cooldown. Authored maps use 5 Mercenary stock / 9 s, 3 Contract Archer / 16 s, 3 Field Medic / 18 s; generated maps default to stock 3 / 16 s. Do not silently normalize those economic gates with unit identity.
5. **Preserve camp budgets.** `CAMP_TEMPLATES` encodes levels, food power, habitats and red/orange/green compositions. Creeps also derive gold bounty and XP from their definitions. A later stat-tier migration should preserve exact values and roster composition, rather than claiming neutral-population merges are balance-free.
6. **Serialized identity and learned skills need deliberate migration.** Commands, snapshots, replay records, issued weapons' `weaponKind`, skill cooldown/autocast maps, campaign variant `base`, cards, ports and public SDK enums all carry kind/ability IDs. Inventory issued weapons inherit their source unit's combat rules. Migrate old IDs at an explicit persisted-format boundary; canonical live state should contain only the retained kind/ability. A mere UI rename leaves the duplication; blindly deleting enum members breaks old records.
7. **Faction appearance must be resolved from ownership.** After a shared-kind merge, `snapshot.players[unit.owner].race` is the source of Grove/Ember clothing, helmet/hood, staff ornament, spell coloration and remains treatment. `appearanceFor` currently accepts only `Pick<GameSnapshot, 'items'>`; extend its input deliberately for the merged rules phase, not during the art-only comparison. Equipment changes the visible loadout without changing the owner's race or body identity. Battlefield, selection portraits, issued-weapon source previews, summons/healing effects and corpses must use the same resolver. A kind such as `summoner` will no longer imply one faction. Keep this proposal out of the current unchanged-rules performance sample.
8. **AI workstream coordination remains required.** Summoner/Pyre Caller are referenced broadly in AI source, route selections and tests. Do not modify frozen AI baselines, strategy routes or their acceptance standards under this proposal. The parent should choose a compatibility/version boundary for the shared game interface before implementing the merges. Read-only audit found approximately 100 AI files mentioning one of the two IDs; implementation is not confined to deleting two catalog rows.

## Validation before accepting a gameplay merge

Use separate commits and saved scenarios for the art-only baseline and the proposed rule changes. For each canonical merge, verify both factions' production buttons/legality, tech gates, supply/upkeep, serialized round trip, replay migration, issued-weapon inheritance and spell cooldown identity. Compare equal-gold and equal-supply engagements, mixed formations and counter relationships. Summoner validation must include manual versus autocast, the 320 company gate, ship-deck spawning, expiry, curse dispel and Chieftain counter; healer validation needs long battles to catch sustained stalemates. Do not adjust acceptance criteria or claim an art performance gain from a changed unit roster.

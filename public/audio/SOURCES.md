# Sound sources

Every recorded sound here is from one of Kenney's sound packs, all released under Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0,
public domain dedication: <http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>). Each pack's own licence file sits in its
folder. Files keep the pack's names; nothing was edited. Downloaded 2026-10-01.

The bow shot, the ship's gun, the cavalry gallop, the spell bolt and the heal, summon and curse sounds are not
recordings: they are made in the browser with WebAudio (see `src/client/sound.ts`).
